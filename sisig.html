FILIPINO FAVORITE

Pork Sisig

A delicious Filipino dish made with chopped pork, onions, chili, and a flavorful savory dressing. Perfect for sharing with family and friends.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 40 minutes
Total Time 1 hour
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork belly
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon calamansi juice
  • 2 pieces green chili, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 egg (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the Pork

    Clean the pork belly and cut it into manageable pieces.

  2. Cook the Pork

    Boil the pork until tender, then drain. Grill or pan-fry it until the outside becomes browned and crispy.

  3. Chop the Pork

    Allow the pork to cool slightly. Chop it into small pieces.

  4. Cook the Aromatics

    Heat oil in a pan. Sauté garlic and onions until fragrant.

  5. Combine the Ingredients

    Add the chopped pork, soy sauce, calamansi juice, and chili. Mix well.

  6. Season and Serve

    Add salt and pepper to taste. Mix in mayonnaise if desired and serve hot.

Chef's Tip

For a more flavorful sisig, make sure the pork is properly browned before chopping. Serve it hot with rice and calamansi on the side.
