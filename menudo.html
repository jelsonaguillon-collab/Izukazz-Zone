Pork Menudo

A traditional Filipino pork stew cooked in tomato sauce with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and flavorful spices. A delicious dish often served during family gatherings.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 40 minutes
Total Time 1 hour
Servings 4–5 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork, cut into small cubes
  • 1 medium potato, diced
  • 1 medium carrot, diced
  • 1 small red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 cup water or broth
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the Ingredients

    Wash and cut the pork, potatoes, carrots, and bell pepper into small pieces.

  2. Sauté the Aromatics

    Heat cooking oil in a pot. Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.

  3. Cook the Pork

    Add the pork and cook until the outside changes color.

  4. Add the Seasonings

    Pour in the soy sauce and tomato sauce. Stir until the pork is coated.

  5. Simmer the Pork

    Add water or broth and the bay leaf. Cover and simmer until the pork becomes tender.

  6. Add the Vegetables

    Add the potatoes and carrots. Cook until they are tender.

  7. Finish and Serve

    Add the bell pepper and season with salt and pepper. Simmer briefly, then serve hot.

Chef's Tip

Cut the vegetables into similar sizes so they cook evenly. Simmer the pork gently until tender for a more flavorful stew. Enjoy your menudo with warm rice.
