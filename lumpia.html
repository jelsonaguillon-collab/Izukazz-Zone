CRISPY FILIPINO FAVORITE

Lumpiang Shanghai

A popular Filipino appetizer made with seasoned ground pork and vegetables wrapped in thin spring roll wrappers and fried until golden and crispy.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 30 minutes
Cooking Time 15 minutes
Total Time 45 minutes
Servings 4–5 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 grams ground pork
  • 20–25 lumpia wrappers
  • 1 small carrot, finely grated
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Cooking oil for frying
  • Water for sealing the wrappers

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the Vegetables

    Wash and finely chop the onion. Grate the carrot and mince the garlic.

  2. Mix the Filling

    In a bowl, combine the ground pork, carrot, onion, garlic, egg, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.

  3. Prepare the Wrappers

    Place one lumpia wrapper on a clean surface. Put a small amount of filling near one edge.

  4. Roll the Lumpia

    Fold the sides inward and roll tightly. Moisten the final edge with water to seal the wrapper.

  5. Heat the Cooking Oil

    Heat cooking oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Carefully place the prepared lumpia into the hot oil.

  6. Fry Until Golden

    Fry in batches, turning as needed, until golden brown and the pork filling is fully cooked.

  7. Drain and Serve

    Remove the lumpia and place them on paper towels to drain excess oil. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.

Chef's Tip

Avoid putting too much filling inside each wrapper. Roll the lumpia tightly and make sure the wrapper is sealed so the filling stays inside while frying. Cook the pork filling completely before serving.
