Beef Caldereta

A classic Filipino beef stew made with tender beef, tomato sauce, potatoes, carrots, and bell peppers. A flavorful dish perfect for family meals.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 1 hour 40 minutes
Total Time 2 hours
Servings 4–5 people
Difficulty Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 750 grams beef, cut into cubes
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups beef broth or water
  • 1 medium potato, cubed
  • 1 medium carrot, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup green peas (optional)
  • 1/4 cup grated cheese (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the Ingredients

    Wash and cut the beef into cubes. Prepare the potatoes, carrots, bell pepper, onion, and garlic.

  2. Brown the Beef

    Heat cooking oil in a pot. Add the beef and cook until the outside is browned.

  3. Sauté the Aromatics

    Add the onion and garlic. Cook until fragrant.

  4. Add the Tomato Sauce

    Stir in the tomato sauce and tomato paste until the beef is evenly coated.

  5. Simmer Until Tender

    Pour in the beef broth or water. Cover and simmer gently until the beef is tender. Add more liquid if needed.

  6. Add the Vegetables

    Add the potatoes and carrots. Cook until almost tender, then add the bell pepper and green peas.

  7. Season and Finish

    Add salt and black pepper to taste. Stir in grated cheese if desired. Simmer until the sauce thickens and the vegetables are cooked.

  8. Serve

    Turn off the heat and serve your beef caldereta while warm.

Chef's Tip

Cook the beef slowly over low heat to make it tender. Check the liquid while simmering and add more broth if necessary. Serve with warm rice.
