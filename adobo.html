Chicken Adobo

A classic Filipino dish known for its delicious combination of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

About This Recipe

Chicken Adobo is one of the most popular Filipino dishes. It is prepared by cooking chicken with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns. The result is a flavorful dish commonly served with steamed rice.

Recipe Information

Information Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 45 minutes
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the chicken by washing and draining it.
  2. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a bowl. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pot over medium heat.
  4. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  5. Pour in the marinade, vinegar, and water.
  6. Add the bay leaves and peppercorns.
  7. Bring the mixture to a boil. Then lower the heat and simmer until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.
  8. Add sugar if desired and adjust the seasoning.
  9. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Cooking Tips

  • Marinate the chicken longer for a stronger flavor.
  • Avoid stirring immediately after adding vinegar.
  • Simmer slowly to make the chicken tender.
  • Serve with hot steamed rice.
