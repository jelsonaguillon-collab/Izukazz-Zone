FILIPINO NOODLE FAVORITE

Pancit Canton

A popular Filipino noodle dish made with stir-fried egg noodles, vegetables, meat, and savory seasonings. Perfect for celebrations and family meals.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 20 minutes
Total Time 35 minutes
Servings 4–5 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 250 grams pancit canton noodles
  • 200 grams chicken breast, sliced
  • 100 grams cabbage, sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, cut into strips
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup green beans, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth or water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Calamansi for serving (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the Ingredients

    Wash and slice the vegetables. Cut the chicken into thin pieces and mince the garlic.

  2. Sauté the Aromatics

    Heat cooking oil in a large pan. Add garlic and onion and cook until fragrant.

  3. Cook the Chicken

    Add the sliced chicken and cook until fully cooked.

  4. Add the Seasonings

    Pour in the soy sauce and oyster sauce. Stir to combine the ingredients.

  5. Add the Broth

    Pour in the chicken broth or water. Bring it to a gentle boil.

  6. Cook the Noodles

    Add the pancit canton noodles. Stir gently and cook according to the package instructions until tender.

  7. Add the Vegetables

    Add the carrots, green beans, and cabbage. Mix and cook until the vegetables are tender but still slightly crisp.

  8. Season and Serve

    Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper. Serve warm with calamansi if desired.

Chef's Tip

Avoid overcooking the noodles. Follow the package instructions and add the vegetables near the end so they keep their color and texture.
