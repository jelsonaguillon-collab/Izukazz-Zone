Fried Chicken

Crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside, fried chicken is a favorite meal for many people.

About This Recipe

Fried chicken is a popular dish made by seasoning chicken, coating it with flour and spices, and frying it until golden brown. It is commonly served with rice, gravy, or your favorite dipping sauce.

Recipe Information

Information Details
Preparation Time 20 minutes
Cooking Time 20–25 minutes
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken pieces
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk or water
  • Cooking oil for frying

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken pieces. Pat them dry.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. In a bowl, combine the flour, cornstarch, and paprika.
  4. In another bowl, beat the egg and mix it with milk or water.
  5. Dip each chicken piece into the egg mixture.
  6. Coat the chicken with the flour mixture.
  7. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  8. Carefully place the chicken into the hot oil. Do not overcrowd the pan.
  9. Fry until golden brown and fully cooked inside. Cooking time depends on the size of the pieces.
  10. Drain the excess oil and allow the chicken to rest briefly before serving.
  11. Serve hot with rice or your favorite dipping sauce.

Cooking Tips

  • Make sure the chicken is fully cooked before serving.
  • Keep the oil at a steady temperature.
  • Avoid overcrowding the pan because it can lower the oil temperature.
  • Use a food thermometer if available. Chicken should reach an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F).
  • Drain the chicken on a rack or paper towels to remove excess oil.
