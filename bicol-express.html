Bicol Express

A spicy Filipino pork dish cooked with coconut milk, chili, and shrimp paste.

About This Recipe

Bicol Express is a popular Filipino dish known for its rich, creamy coconut milk sauce and spicy flavor. It is traditionally prepared with pork, chili peppers, and shrimp paste.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 40 minutes
Total Time 55 minutes
Servings 4–5 people
Difficulty Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork belly, cut into small pieces
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 cup coconut cream
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons shrimp paste (bagoong)
  • 3–5 green chili peppers, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Wash and cut the pork into small pieces. Chop the onion and garlic, then slice the chili peppers.
  2. Sauté the ingredients. Heat cooking oil in a pan. Add garlic and onion and cook until fragrant.
  3. Cook the pork. Add the pork pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add shrimp paste. Mix in the shrimp paste and stir until the pork is coated.
  5. Pour in coconut milk. Add coconut milk and let the mixture simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  6. Add chili peppers and coconut cream. Stir in the chili peppers and coconut cream. Continue cooking until the sauce becomes thick.
  7. Season and serve. Add salt and pepper if needed. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Chef's Tip

Adjust the amount of chili peppers depending on how spicy you want your Bicol Express. Stir occasionally while simmering to prevent the coconut milk from sticking.
