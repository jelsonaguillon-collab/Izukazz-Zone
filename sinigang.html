Sinigang

A traditional Filipino sour soup made with meat, fresh vegetables, and a flavorful sour broth.

About This Recipe

Sinigang is a well-loved Filipino soup known for its distinct sour taste. It is commonly prepared with pork, fish, shrimp, or beef, together with vegetables. Tamarind is often used to give the soup its sour flavor.

Recipe Information

Information Details
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 45–60 minutes
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork belly or pork ribs
  • 1 packet tamarind soup mix
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach)
  • 5 pieces okra
  • 6 cups water
  • Fish sauce to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare and wash all the vegetables and pork.
  2. Place the pork in a pot with water.
  3. Add the onion and tomatoes.
  4. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  5. Add the tamarind soup mix and stir until dissolved.
  6. Add the radish, eggplant, and okra.
  7. Cook until the vegetables become tender.
  8. Add the kangkong and cook for a few more minutes.
  9. Season with fish sauce according to your taste.
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Cooking Tips

  • Simmer the pork until tender before adding the vegetables.
  • Avoid overcooking the vegetables so they keep their texture.
  • Adjust the sourness according to your preference.
  • Serve Sinigang while hot for the best experience.
